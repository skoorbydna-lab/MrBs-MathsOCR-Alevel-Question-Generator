A-Level Maths Past Exam Question Selector

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open index.html in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
Keep index.html, data.js, similar.js, search.js and the img and similar folders together.

Searching: type in the search box above the questions (e.g. hypothesis test, Poisson,
"significance level"). It searches every course at once, using chapter names, topics and the
wording of each question. Use the Course (Maths / Further Maths) and Year (12 / 13) buttons on
the left to narrow the results. Press Esc or clear the box to go back to browsing by chapter.

To add papers: send them to Claude, who will give you an update zip. Unzip it over this folder
and say yes to replace the files.
